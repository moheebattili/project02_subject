The CSS Box Model Math

content-box (Default)

Declared Width: 300px

Padding: 20px (left & right = +40px)

Border: 10px (left & right = +20px)

Rendered Width: 360px

Padding and borders are added on top of the declared width, breaking tight layout constraints.

border-box (Recommended)

Declared Width: 300px

Padding: 20px (left & right = absorbed)

Border: 10px (left & right = absorbed)

Rendered Width: 300px

Padding and borders are absorbed inside the declared width, ensuring predictable layouts.
